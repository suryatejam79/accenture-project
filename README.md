# Social Buzz Popularity Analysis – Accenture Virtual Project

## Project Overview
This project was completed as part of the Accenture Data Analytics & Visualization Virtual Experience Program offered by Forage. The task involved working with a simulated client, Social Buzz, a fast-growing technology unicorn preparing for global scale and a potential IPO.

### Objective
To analyze over 100,000 simulated social media posts per day and identify the top 5 most popular content categories, providing data-backed insights to support IPO readiness and content strategy.

---

## Problem Statement
Social Buzz receives an overwhelming amount of user-generated content daily. The company needed a solution to:
- Identify the most engaging content categories
- Understand monthly engagement trends
- Use insights to support their growth and IPO plans
